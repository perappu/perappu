## Hello! 🦜

I am known by many names, but you'll probably most often hear me called Toko! I'm a web developer and occasional multimedia artist.

Namely, I am a maintainer for the open source Laravel-based framework [Lorekeeper](https://github.com/perappu/lorekeeper). I also made the Dalamud C# plugin [WhoSaidWhatNow](https://github.com/perappu/WhoSaidWhatNow) and the SvelteKit headless CMS project [Saussure](https://github.com/perappu/saussure). You'll also find a lot of various Python toys and doo-dads I've made over the years.

I mostly work in PHP as part of my Laravel work, but I also do a solid amount of Typescript, C#, and Python.

You can view some blog posts and other information about me on [my website](https://perappu.blog). Thanks for looking at my work!
